Prediction markets

A market asks a question that will be resolved by a stated deadline. People trade shares tied to each possible outcome.

  • YES and NO are opposite outcomes; check the exact resolution rules before trading.
  • A share price can suggest the crowd's implied odds, but it is not a guarantee or an objective probability.
  • Prices move as people trade. Thin liquidity can make prices jump, and you can lose what you put in.

Monad, in a nutshell

Monad is an EVM-compatible layer-1 blockchain. Ethereum-style smart contracts and familiar developer tools can be used on it.

  • MonadBFT is the consensus protocol validators use to agree on transaction order.
  • Parallel execution can process independent transactions at the same time while preserving correct results.
  • Its pipelined execution architecture and MonadDb are designed to support high-throughput applications.

Using the Monad network

Wallets sign transactions; validators process them. On-chain transactions use gas, paid in Monad's native token, MON.

  • This demo is configured for Monad Testnet (chain ID 10143). Testnet assets are for testing, not mainnet funds.
  • Wallet connection here does not make trades on-chain; market fills are local simulations.
  • Never share your recovery phrase or private key. Verify the network and website before signing anything.
